# Trial Balance

## Purpose

The Trial Balance Business Object represents the summarized balances of all Ledgers at a specific point in time.

It verifies that the Accounting Engine satisfies the principles of Double Entry Accounting by ensuring that total Debit balances equal total Credit balances.

The Trial Balance is the foundation for all financial statements, including the Profit & Loss Statement and Balance Sheet.

The Trial Balance is a calculated Business Object and must never be maintained manually.

---

# Responsibilities

The Trial Balance is responsible for:

* Summarizing Ledger balances.
* Verifying accounting accuracy.
* Supporting financial reporting.
* Providing balances for Profit & Loss.
* Providing balances for Balance Sheet.
* Detecting accounting imbalances.

The Trial Balance is **not** responsible for:

* Creating Journal Entries.
* Posting Journal Lines.
* Managing Ledgers.
* Calculating GST.
* Importing accounting data.

# Validation Rules

* Financial Period is mandatory.
* Reporting Date is mandatory.
* Every Ledger appears at most once.
* Total Debit must equal Total Credit.

---

# Business Rules

## Rule 1

Trial Balance is calculated entirely from posted Journal Lines.

---

## Rule 2

Draft or Unposted Journal Entries must not affect the Trial Balance.

---

## Rule 3

Every Ledger with activity during the reporting period appears in the Trial Balance.

Inactive Ledgers with no balance may be omitted depending on reporting preferences.

---

## Rule 4

Total Debits must always equal Total Credits.

```text id="q4brx4"
Total Debit

=

Total Credit
```

If this rule fails, accounting integrity has been violated.

---

## Rule 5

Users cannot manually modify Trial Balance values.

The only way to change the Trial Balance is by posting or reversing Journal Entries.

---

## Rule 6

The Trial Balance is generated for a specific Financial Period and reporting date.

Historical Trial Balances remain reproducible.

---

## Rule 7

Deleting Trial Balance data is prohibited.

Historical financial reports must remain reproducible from Journal Lines.

# Guiding Principle

**The Trial Balance is a financial checkpoint, not a data entry object.**

It is a derived representation of all posted accounting activity and serves as the definitive validation that the Accounting Engine remains balanced, auditable, and financially accurate. Every financial statement within AaramBooks ultimately originates from the Trial Balance.

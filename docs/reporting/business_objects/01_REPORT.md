# Report

## Purpose

The Report Business Object represents a logical collection of business information generated from canonical data maintained throughout the AaramBooks platform.

A Report provides users with structured, meaningful, and actionable information by querying data from one or more business domains.

Reports support operational monitoring, inventory analysis, financial reporting, compliance, and executive decision-making.

A Report is a read-only business object.

It never creates, modifies, or deletes business data.

---

# Responsibilities

The Report Business Object is responsible for:

* Presenting business information.
* Aggregating data across domains.
* Applying business filters.
* Supporting grouping and summarization.
* Providing sortable datasets.
* Supporting exports.
* Providing data for dashboards.
* Supporting scheduled reporting.
* Providing drill-down capabilities.

The Report is **not** responsible for:

* Creating business documents.
* Updating inventory.
* Posting accounting transactions.
* Importing external files.
* Matching operational documents.
* Executing business workflows.

---

# Design Philosophy

A Report is **not** a database query.

It represents a business concept.

Examples include:

* Sales Report
* Inventory Balance Report
* Warehouse Stock Report
* Pending Settlement Report
* GST Summary
* Trial Balance
* Profit & Loss
* Matching Exception Report

Each Report should answer a specific business question.

# Validation Rules

* Report Code must be unique.
* Report Name must be unique.
* Report Category is mandatory.
* Report Type is mandatory.
* Source Domain must be specified.
* Template is mandatory.
* Status is mandatory.

---

# Business Rules

## Rule 1

Reports are always read-only.

---

## Rule 2

Reports consume only committed business data.

Draft or incomplete transactions must not appear unless explicitly requested.

---

## Rule 3

Reports must produce deterministic results.

The same filters applied to the same data must always produce identical output.

---

## Rule 4

Reports may consume data from multiple domains simultaneously.

Example:

Sales Report

↓

Operations

*

Inventory

*

Accounting

---

## Rule 5

Business calculations belong to upstream domains.

Reports must not duplicate business logic.

---

## Rule 6

Reports may support pagination for large datasets.

---

## Rule 7

Inactive Reports cannot be generated by users.

---

## Rule 8

Deleting Reports is prohibited.

Reports may only be marked as Inactive.

# Performance Requirements

The Reporting Engine should satisfy the following objectives:

* Fast report generation.
* Efficient pagination.
* Lazy loading where applicable.
* Optimized database queries.
* Minimal memory usage.
* Support for large datasets.

Future versions may introduce caching for frequently accessed reports.

---

# Security

Every Report must enforce:

* User authentication.
* Role-based authorization.
* Company isolation.
* Warehouse restrictions.
* Financial access controls.

Users should only view information they are authorized to access.

# Guiding Principle

**A Report transforms trusted business data into trusted business information.**

It exists to answer business questions accurately, consistently, and efficiently while remaining completely independent of transactional processing and business logic.
